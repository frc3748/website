Update #1 (4/1)

As promised, we have been hard at work making the final plans for the District Championships. A few things have changed to accommodate a larger group based on the hotel availability. The cost of the trip will be $250/student. This covers the hotel, all meals, and our teacher sponsors. If you plan to travel with your student and secure your own hotel or will be attending only a portion of the trip, just let us know so we can adjust costs.

This can be paid by check to Mt. Hebron High School with a note "Ragnarok Robotics." We are also looking to set up the OSP again for payment but will need to wait until school is back in session.

If the cost of the trip is a financial burden, please contact Trish Ververs directly. We can help cover the trip out of this year's grant. We do not want the trip cost to be a deterrent to attending this important event.

The next step is to secure an accurate count of the students that plan to attend. We will be sending out permission slips that will need to be signed and returned as soon as possible. In the meantime, if a parent/guardian can contact us via email to let us know your plans so that we can secure the right number of rooms — please include any special circumstances.

A total of 2–3 same-gender students will be grouped together in each hotel room. Parents do NOT need to book hotel rooms for their students — the hotel rooms have already been booked and are covered by the cost of the field trip. Meal Information

All meals during the tournament will be provided and are covered in the cost of the field trip. It is recommended that students and parents plan for meals before arriving and after leaving the event.
