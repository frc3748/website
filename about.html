The Story

Since our rookie debut in 2011, Ragnarok Robotics has evolved into a powerhouse of engineering and competitive consistency within the FIRST Robotics Competition. Over the past decade, we have grown from a promising new team into a perennial contender.

Our recent seasons demonstrate our sustained momentum. In both 2022 and 2023, the team qualified for the District Championship, cementing our status as one of the top-performing teams in the region.

The 2023 season was particularly distinguished by our receipt of the Armistead Engineering Innovation Award, recognizing not just our ability to compete, but our ability to design creative, robust, and professional-grade industrial solutions.

Our Mission

To inspire young people to be science and technology leaders and innovators, by engaging them in exciting mentor-based programs that build science, engineering, and technology skills.
